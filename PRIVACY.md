# Privacy Policy — JatinSitDown

_Last updated: 2026. This is a plain-language policy for a local-first app. It is
a starting point, not legal advice — have a professional review it before a
commercial launch._

## The short version

**Your practice data stays on your device.** JatinSitDown is designed so it can
run entirely in your browser, with no account and no server. We don't want your
data — we want you to practice.

## What we store, and where

- **On your device only (browser storage / IndexedDB):** your streak, practice
  history, skill progress, songs you add, and settings. This never leaves your
  device unless you turn on optional sync (below). Clearing your browser data or
  uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Microphone:** if you turn on mic input, audio is processed **in real time in
  your browser** to detect the note you played. Audio is **not recorded, stored,
  or transmitted** anywhere.
- **MIDI keyboard:** note data from a connected keyboard is used only to score
  your playing, in your browser. It is not stored or transmitted.

## Optional cloud sync (off by default)

If you choose to enable sync (by configuring Supabase), your practice data is
stored in **your own** Supabase project so your devices can share progress. In
that case that data is handled under Supabase's terms and your project's
settings. Sync is **off unless you set it up** — the default app sends nothing.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, no selling data, no third-party tracking cookies.
- No account required to use the app.
- No analytics that identify you. (If privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics are
  added later, this policy will say so.)

## Notifications

If you enable practice reminders, the app uses your browser's notification
system locally. No reminder content is sent to a server.

## Children

The app is for general audiences. It collects no personal information by
default. If you enable cloud sync for a child, you are responsible for that
account.

## Your control

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete all of it any time by
clearing the app's site data or uninstalling it. If you enabled sync, you can
also delete it from your Supabase project.
